## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Farmers often receive low prices for their products because of multiple intermediaries. Consumers, on the other hand, pay higher prices for agricultural products. Transportation costs and poor supply chain management also create problems.

## Proposed Solution
We propose a digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers. This platform helps farmers sell their products at better prices and allows consumers to buy fresh products at affordable prices.

## Technical Approach
Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Backend: Python Flask
Database: MySQL
AI/ML: Python, Scikit-learn
Maps: OpenStreetMap

## Feasibility and Viability
The proposed system can be developed using web technologies, a database, and AI tools. Farmers and buyers can use the platform through a website or mobile device.

Initially, the project can be tested with a small number of farmers and buyers. Challenges such as internet connectivity, transportation costs, and lack of technical knowledge can be addressed through a simple interface, local delivery partners, and regional language support.

The platform can be expanded to support more farmers, products, and locations in the future.

## Impact and Benefits
Farmers: Better market access and opportunities to earn more.
Consumers: Fresh agricultural products at competitive prices.
Cost Reduction: Fewer unnecessary intermediaries and lower transportation costs.
Time Saving: Faster ordering and delivery coordination.
Environmental Benefits: Reduced food waste and unnecessary transportation.
Supply Chain Efficiency: Better demand forecasting and product distribution.
